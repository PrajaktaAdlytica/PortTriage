Background and motion decisions

Cool white creates contrast with white evidence cards; luminous water supplies depth to ownership, audience and closing sections. Deep navy ends the story. Ocean motion uses a slowly transformed image layer, pauses offscreen, respects reduced motion and can be paused from the footer.

  • web.dev animation guidance: prefer transform and opacity; avoid unnecessary layout work.
  • GSAP accessibility and matchMedia: adapt motion to user preferences.
  • MDN media queries for accessibility.
